# Figure Narrative and Editorial Logic

## Guiding principle

Every figure should answer **one biological question**.

Each subsequent figure should naturally arise from the previous one, progressively revealing how fungal antagonism unfolds from global transcriptional changes to functional reprogramming and extracellular mechanisms.

Panels are included only if they provide **new biological evidence**. Redundant visualizations, even if common in transcriptomic studies, should be avoided.

# Figure 3 — Global and spatial transcriptional responses

### Biological question

How extensive, reproducible, and spatially structured are the transcriptional responses during fungal confrontation?

### Biological answer

Both fungi exhibit robust transcriptional responses to interaction. *Ophiostoma novo-ulmi* displays a substantially broader response than *Fusarium cf. salinense*, and the inclusion of a non-interaction sampling zone reveals that pathogen transcriptional reprogramming consists of both localized and colony-wide responses.

## Panel A

**Question**

Does *Fusarium* exhibit a reproducible transcriptional response?

**Answer**

Principal component analysis demonstrates clear separation between interaction and self-interaction samples.

## Panel B

**Question**

Does *Ophiostoma* exhibit reproducible and biologically distinct transcriptional states?

**Answer**

Principal component analysis separates self-interaction, non-interaction, and interaction samples into distinct expression states.

## Panel C

**Question**

How extensive and directionally balanced are the transcriptional responses?

**Answer**

A diverging bar plot summarizes significantly differentially expressed genes (padj < 0.05), separated into induced and repressed genes.

**Purpose**

- Quantify transcriptome-wide responses.
- Compare transcriptional response magnitude.
- Compare transcriptional direction.
- Demonstrate the broader transcriptional reprogramming of *Ophiostoma*.

## Panel D

**Question**

Are *Ophiostoma* transcriptional responses confined to the interaction zone?

**Answer**

A large expression heatmap identifies interaction-specific, colony-wide, and gradient-like transcriptional responses across the three sampling regions.

**Purpose**

- Visualize spatial organization of transcription.
- Distinguish local versus global responses.
- Justify the inclusion of the third experimental condition.
- Introduce the biological complexity explored in subsequent figures.

# Figure 4 — Functional reprogramming during fungal interaction

### Biological question

How do the two fungi differ in their functional transcriptional programs during confrontation?

### Biological answer

Although both fungi undergo extensive transcriptional reprogramming, they activate distinct functional programs. Comparative COG analysis identifies biological processes that respond similarly as well as categories exhibiting species-specific regulation.

## Panel A

**Question**

Which functional categories respond in *Fusarium*?

**Answer**

COG categories significantly enriched among differentially expressed *Fusarium* genes.

## Panel B

**Question**

Which functional categories respond in *Ophiostoma*?

**Answer**

COG categories significantly enriched among differentially expressed *Ophiostoma* genes.

## Panel C

**Question**

Which functional responses differ between fungi?

**Answer**

Species × COG interaction analysis identifies functional categories exhibiting significantly different transcriptional responses between the two fungi.

**Purpose**

- Directly compare both organisms.
- Identify conserved versus divergent functional strategies.
- Transition from global transcriptional changes to specific biological processes.

# Figure 5 — Extracellular responses during fungal antagonism

### Biological question

Which extracellular functions characterize fungal antagonism?

### Biological answer

Both fungi regulate extracellular proteins during interaction, but *Fusarium cf. salinense* allocates a larger proportion of its response to predicted secreted proteins and secreted CAZymes, whereas *Ophiostoma novo-ulmi* exhibits a broader extracellular transcriptional response involving a larger diversity of CAZyme families.

## Panel A

**Question**

How much of the transcriptional response is devoted to extracellular functions?

**Answer**

Comparative overview of significant secreted proteins, high-confidence CAZymes, and secreted CAZymes.

## Panel B

**Question**

Which CAZyme classes contribute most strongly to extracellular remodeling?

**Answer**

Comparative visualization of the major CAZyme classes, highlighting induced and repressed extracellular responses.

## Panel C

**Question**

Which extracellular enzyme families dominate fungal interaction?

**Answer**

Comparative analysis of the principal secreted CAZyme families differentially regulated during confrontation.

**Purpose**

- Identify candidate enzymes associated with fungal antagonism.
- Compare extracellular investment between fungi.
- Connect functional categories to candidate molecular mechanisms.

# Editorial principle

Design figures around biological questions rather than analytical methods.

For every proposed panel, ask:

> **What new biological question does this panel answer?**

If the answer is unclear or already provided elsewhere, the panel should be removed.
